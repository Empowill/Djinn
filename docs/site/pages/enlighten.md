# Enlighten me

**Enlighten me**, beside Rub the lamp on a question's card, asks for more before you decide. A worker digs into what
your note asks, then the question is revised and waits for you again. Meanwhile it folds to one line, **Being
investigated**, and you may still decide.

> **This page is a sketch.** Its full guide, how to use it from the window and from the Djinn, what to expect and
> the common mistakes, is being written.
