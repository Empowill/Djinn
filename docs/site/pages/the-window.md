# The window

The side panel holds **New wish**, the **flying carpet**, which shows every active wish together with what waits for
you first, the **Hourglass**, the machine's live metrics, then your wishes and your projects. A wish shows its tabs:
Wish, Tasks, Review, Decisions, Tilasms and For agents. A terminal sits at the bottom, with the Djinn's tab.

The **personal space**, at the bottom of the side panel, keeps your own settings: the language, the theme and the
text size. Compact and Very compact shrink the whole window, its spacing with its text, to see more at once; Large
grows it. The documentation opens in the window's theme and text size.

> **This page is a sketch.** Its full guide, how to use it from the window and from the Djinn, what to expect and
> the common mistakes, is being written.
