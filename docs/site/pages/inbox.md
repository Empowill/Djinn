# Inbox

The **Inbox**, at the top of the flying carpet, holds what waits for you outside any wish: the requests a source found,
and a source of this machine that fails. Empty, it is hidden. Nothing is made until you click, and Djinn only reads a
source, never answers it.

## A card in three sentences

Every card says three things, always in this order:

1. **What it is.** Its kind first, **Request** or **Djinn's configuration**, then the fact: the merge request assigned
   to you, the source that no longer runs.
2. **What Djinn will do**, before you click: for a request, what the recommended choice starts, a wish that follows
   it with its skill's watcher, a block filed in a wish, a new wish whose lead plans the work.
3. **What each choice changes**, under its name. No button stands alone: the recommended choice is marked, and
   **Rub the lamp** takes it.

A request's choices are where Djinn proposes to send it, the recommended one first, then **Dismiss**: it will not come
back, and nothing is said or marked at the source.

## Sources

A skill may declare a source: a command that prints what comes from outside, like the merge requests assigned to you.
It says what it reads, in a sentence its skill writes in its metadata, else the skill's description. The sources live
in their project's panel, under **Inbox sources, on this machine**: what each one reads, how its last run went, and
**Plug in** or **Unplug**, with what that does written under the button. A source runs only once you plug it in, on
this machine; the inbox never lists the sources.

## A source that fails

A source you plugged in whose runs fail gets a card of its own: since when, how many runs failed in a row, and the
error its command gave. While it fails, what it reads no longer comes. Djinn runs it again on its own, and the first
run that goes well takes the card away. Its two ways out: fix the cause on this machine, recommended (a tool to log in
to, a missing command), or **Unplug it on this machine**; its items stay. A source whose skill Djinn cannot read gets
the same card, until the skill is fixed.
