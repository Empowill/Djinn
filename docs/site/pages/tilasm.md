# Tilasm

A tilasm is the material that explains a wish: a page, a diagram, a demonstration, coded L01, L02 and on, versioned,
and kept in Djinn's data folder, never in a project. The wish's **Tilasms** tab opens one in a frame of its own, where
its scripts run and the network and Djinn stay out of its reach. You may drop a folder or a zip file on the tab to
make one.

> **This page is a sketch.** Its full guide, how to use it from the window and from the Djinn, what to expect and
> the common mistakes, is being written.
