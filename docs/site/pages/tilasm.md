# Tilasm

A tilasm is the material that explains a wish: a page, a diagram, a demonstration, coded L01, L02 and on, versioned,
and kept in Djinn's data folder, never in a project. The wish's **Tilasms** tab opens one in a frame of its own, where
its scripts run and the network and Djinn stay out of its reach. You may drop a folder or a zip file on the tab to
make one.

## Comments

Read a tilasm and say what it calls for where you read it. **Comments** (the speech bubble on its head, with the count
of your drafts) opens a column beside the frame, full size too; **On the whole tilasm** writes a comment on the
whole of it. A comment is a draft: it stays in Djinn's store on this machine, survives a restart, and no agent reads
it. Edit one in place (Ctrl+Enter saves, Escape cancels); delete it at once, and **Undo** puts it back for five
seconds. Your drafts are counted in gold on the tilasm's head, on its row and on the **Tilasms** tab.

Comments on a passage or an element of the page, and sending your drafts to the Djinn in one go, come next.

> **This page is a sketch.** Its full guide, how to use it from the window and from the Djinn, what to expect and
> the common mistakes, is being written.
