# Review

The **Review** tab lists a wish's merged work, a line per task, and counts what is left to review. A click opens a
line's diff, with the comments on its lines; its box marks it reviewed, kept with who checked it and when, which the
Djinn reads.

> **This page is a sketch.** Its full guide, how to use it from the window and from the Djinn, what to expect and
> the common mistakes, is being written.
