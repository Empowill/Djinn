# Decision

A decision is an answered question, or a decision the Djinn wrote down. The **Decisions** tab lists every one, the
latest first, read only: who took it, you, the Djinn or a worker, and the tasks it led to.

> **This page is a sketch.** Its full guide, how to use it from the window and from the Djinn, what to expect and
> the common mistakes, is being written.
