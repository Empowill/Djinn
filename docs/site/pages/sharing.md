# Sharing a wish

**Share**, at the top of a wish, writes it to one file in your Downloads folder: its plan, questions, decisions,
blocks, tilasms and journal. Never code, never a secret, never a local path. **Import a wish**, in the side panel,
opens it on another machine, each project found by its remote, then by its name.

> **This page is a sketch.** Its full guide, how to use it from the window and from the Djinn, what to expect and
> the common mistakes, is being written.
