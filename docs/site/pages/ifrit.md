# Ifrit

An ifrit is a worker that stays lit while you are there, and that Djinn puts out after a while without you. It is
landing in Djinn now: this page will explain it once it has.

It keeps its [gates](#gates) while it is lit, with no hour limit: they go back when it goes out or ends. Who waits for
one is told it is released when you leave, and at what time at the latest.

> **This page is a sketch.** Its full guide, how to use it from the window and from the Djinn, what to expect and
> the common mistakes, is being written.
