# T29 · Collaborate on a wish

**Goal.** Several people work together on the same wish, bringing answers and comments from the online page back into the wish.

## Design
- Several people on one wish, even on one branch: answers and comments from the page come back
  into the wish, through the lead, never by editing the page.
- We hand off today; working at the same time comes with collaboration.
- The word is `sync`: `djinn wish sync <wish>`. One way today (Djinn to the page), both ways
  once people collaborate.

## Open questions
- How a comment on the page becomes an answer in the wish, safely.

## Done when
- [ ] Several people collaborate on the same wish online, their answers and comments returning to the wish. (needs: two people and an online wish)
